Guide 01

Cozy Traditional

So, hey VK. I've been looking through your board, and I'm noticing a few themes. There are a lot of formal looking living rooms with white painted crown molding and wall details. I'm seeing white slipcovered sofas, rounded lamps, layered gallery walls with original artwork, and plenty of fresh flowers. Overall, it feels elegant, a little Nancy Meyers, and a bit more mature. It's a style I associate with our parents' generation. Because of that, it also feels classic and timeless. Everything is bright, filled with natural light, and has a comfortable, lived in quality. One challenge is that almost all of your inspiration photos feature white painted wood interiors. Your home has dark wood paneled walls, and I know painting them isn't an option. So instead of trying to recreate these rooms exactly, we'll need to find colors, materials, and furniture that capture the same feeling while working with what you already have.

01 / The style

How I'd describe it

What it is

  • Timelessly fashionable, leaning on classic traditional elements
  • gallery walls full of original artwork
  • Eclectic and lived-in, warm
  • Ample traditional looking lamps, large-scale flower arrangements
  • slip covers

What it isn't

  • trendy or trying to reference a specific era
  • gallery walls full of digital art / posters
  • minimalist, sterlie
  • Modern statement lamps, anything "edgy"
  • leather
