how to hang & light your star

It's simple: two screws, a pendant cord and a bulb.

01

What's in the box

  • Your star sconce
  • A paper hanging guide that shows exactly where to drill your two screws
02

What you'll need

  • Two screws (plus wall anchors if you're going into drywall), or any hanging method you like
  • A plug-in pendant cord with a socket and an on/off switch
  • A light bulb

cord I recommend

Cord pick + link (e.g. a UL-listed cloth plug-in pendant cord from Color Cord Co.)

bulb I recommend

Bulb pick + link. A warm white (2700K) LED stays cool behind the ceramic. Note the max size that fits.

03

How to hang it

The bulb sits behind the star, so you never see it directly. The light spills out around the points and fans across the wall.

  1. Tape the paper hanging guide to the wall where you want your star, and drill where it shows.
  2. Put in your two screws, leaving them sticking out a little.
  3. Screw the bulb into your pendant cord and set it in place behind the star.
  4. Hang the sconce on the screws. It sits flush against the wall.
  5. Plug it in and switch it on.

To change the bulb, just lift the star off the screws.

A little heads upIf you're standing right along the wall next to it, you might catch a peek of the bulb, so it can look a little bright from that angle. From the front, you only see the glow.
