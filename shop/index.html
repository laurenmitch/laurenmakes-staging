the fine print

How does the 10-minute hold work?

When you click "Hold it & check out," that sconce is reserved just for you and a timer starts. Anyone else sees it as held. If you finish paying, it's yours. If the timer runs out, it goes back in the shop for the next person. Nobody can ever be charged for a piece someone else is holding.

Where do you ship, and how much is it?

The US and Canada for now: $18 US · $38 Canada (sample rates). Every sconce is double-boxed. Any import fees into Canada are paid by the buyer on delivery.

What bulb and fitting does it take?

Fill in: plug-in cord or hardwired, socket size, cord length and recommended bulb.
