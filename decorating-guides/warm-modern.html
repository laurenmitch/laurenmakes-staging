Guide 06

Warm Modern

This home is modern, but it's a bold modern: clean and intentional without ever tipping into minimalism. It’s warm and lit from the side with geometric sculptural lamps, and folk art hung next to bold posters and built-ins full of books. There is clean color everywhere, and still room to breathe.

01 / Your style

How I'd describe it

What it is

  • Bold modern, unafraid of color
  • Raw, light wood tones and velvet
  • Unique modern furniture with vintage and folk art mixed in
  • Clean and intentional
  • Full of life, color, and objects

What it isn't

  • Beige modern
  • Dark heavy wood, fussy or ornate lighting
  • A matching set that all arrived at once
  • Minimalist or sparse
  • Maximalist or cluttered

That's it folks.
